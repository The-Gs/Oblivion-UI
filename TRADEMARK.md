This split is intentional. The code is free for anyone to use, modify, and
redistribute — including in commercial products. The name exists so that users
can tell what is genuinely Oblivion UI and what isn't.

## What you may do without asking

- Use Oblivion UI in your website, app, or product — commercial or not, at any scale
- Say your product "uses Oblivion UI", is "built with Oblivion UI", or is
  "compatible with Oblivion UI" (nominative fair use)
- Write tutorials, courses, blog posts, or videos about Oblivion UI, including
  paid ones
- Fork the code and distribute your fork **under a different name**
- Publish themes, plugins, or extensions named `<yourname>-for-oblivion-ui`

## What requires written permission

- Naming your own product, library, npm package, or company using the Marks
- Selling a product whose name incorporates the Marks — for example, a paid
  component pack marketed as "Oblivion UI Pro" or "Oblivion UI Premium"
- Using the Marks in a way that implies Oblivion UI endorses, sponsors, or
  maintains your product
- Using the logo as your own product's logo or app icon
- Registering domain names, social handles, or npm scopes built around the Marks

## Forks

You are free to fork. If your fork is more than a trivial patch set, give it its
own name and make clear on first mention that it is derived from, but not the
same as, Oblivion UI. Do not present a fork as the official project.
